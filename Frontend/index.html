VISITOR HUB · ABOUT

A better welcome,
from the very first step.

A simple digital check-in experience for guests visiting DY Patil International University.

01 ABOUT THE APPLICATION

Campus visits,
made easy.

Visitor Hub helps guests share their visit details before arriving on campus. Email verification keeps the process connected to the visitor, and a QR pass makes the final check-in easy to present at the entrance.

The application is designed around one clear goal: helping every guest feel expected and welcome at DYPIU.

01✉

Email verification

A one-time code confirms the visitor’s email before details are submitted.

02▤

Simple registration

Share your name, address, and reason for visiting in one short form.

03▦

QR visitor pass

Receive a QR pass after successful registration and keep it ready for arrival.
